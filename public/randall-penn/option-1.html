The work behind the design
The part you cannot see in a screenshot

A professional site needs more than a professional appearance.

The Essential website includes the important unseen work required to launch properly. Its SEO foundations help search engines understand the content, while its technology and booking setup help the website function reliably. The absence of animation does not mean the absence of technical work.

SEO & AEO, explained simply

Think of SEO as clear signage for search engines. It tells them what each page is about and helps them discover it. AEO makes information on the site easier for AI-powered answer tools to interpret. Neither can guarantee a particular search result.

Essential includes basic SEO and AEO. Google Analytics, Search Console, structured data enhancements and the additional local SEO work belong to Professional and Complete.

What SEO setup means

We build and optimise the agreed website pages so search engines can better understand and access them. Google ultimately determines whether, when and where a page appears in results, and search performance can change over time.

What isn't part of the build fee

Ongoing monthly SEO, regular analytics reporting, paid advertising, professional photography or videography, major logo redesigns and additional custom pages are not included unless separately agreed. Domain, hosting and Microsoft 365 subscriptions are external costs.

What we'll need from you

Your existing logo, verified business and service information, approved project photographs and descriptions, social links, any testimonials and applicable licences, plus the access needed for the agreed integrations. We'll review these together before development begins.

How delivery works

Estimated build: approximately 3 weeks once the deposit, external costs, content and required access are received. The proposal specifies 50% deposit and 50% before launch. Hosting, domain, Microsoft 365 and professional photography are not included in the quoted build fee.
