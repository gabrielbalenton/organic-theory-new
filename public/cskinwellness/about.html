Care for skin. Care for the person.

A little more about me

I’m Czarina, a professional esthetician and founder of C Skin Care & Wellness in Alameda, California. I’ve always loved health, beauty, and the small rituals that help people feel more confident and comfortable in their own skin.

Growing up, my mom owned a boutique shop. I remember watching her get ready for work each morning. Once she had taken that little bit of time for herself, she seemed to stand a little taller. That stayed with me.

Later, through more than two decades of working with older adults, I saw that same idea from another perspective. Sometimes a haircut, a little makeup, getting dressed up to see friends, or simply feeling cared for could completely brighten someone’s day.

Those experiences shaped how I think about skin care today. For me, it is not only about how skin looks. It is also about confidence, consistency, feeling cared for, and building habits that support how you want to look and feel over time.

Rooted in Alameda

Alameda is home. My family lives here, my kids go to school here, and I am proud to build C Skin Care & Wellness in the same community we are part of every day. I want this studio to be a trusted local place where people feel comfortable, welcomed, and known.

Skincare through different stages of life

Healthy-looking skin does not belong to one age group, and our needs naturally evolve over time. Whether your focus is congestion, pigmentation, sensitivity, hydration, maintenance, or well-aging, your visit begins with understanding what your skin needs now.

Skincare that feels personal

We take time to understand your concerns, your routine, your goals, and what feels realistic for your life. No rushed appointments. No one-size-fits-all approach. Just thoughtful, personalized care designed around you.
